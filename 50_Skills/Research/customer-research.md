# Customer Research

## Purpose

Discover what customers actually think, say, struggle with, and seek—using existing assets and source-backed online research rather than invented personas.

## When to activate

- customer research
- voice of customer
- voc
- customer interview
- survey analysis
- review mining
- reddit research
- jtbd
- why customers churn
- persona research

## Inputs required

- Research goal
- Target segment
- Existing transcripts/surveys/tickets/reviews
- Product/business context
- Allowed external sources

## Workflow

1. Choose mode: analyze existing assets, gather new evidence, or both.
2. Extract JTBD, pains, triggers, desired outcomes, exact language, alternatives, and objections.
3. Preserve verbatim quotes with source/date/context.
4. Cluster themes and score frequency × intensity.
5. Segment before generalizing; check sample bias and recency.
6. Label confidence based on independent evidence.
7. Build personas only after enough consistent data points.
8. Identify research gaps and the cheapest next evidence.

## Outputs

- Research synthesis
- VOC quote bank
- JTBD map
- Evidence-backed personas
- Research gap analysis

## Quality gates

- [ ] No invented quote or persona detail
- [ ] Single-source insight cannot be presented as universal
- [ ] Contradictions preserved
- [ ] Source, date, and segment context retained

- When **strategy** dominates → go to **Product Strategy**.
- When **competitive view** dominates → go to **Competitor Analysis**.
