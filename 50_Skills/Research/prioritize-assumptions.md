# Prioritize Assumptions

## Purpose

Identify assumptions that must be true, score uncertainty and impact, and choose the cheapest credible test before committing heavily.

## When to activate

- prioritize assumptions
- assumption mapping
- riskiest assumption
- test assumptions
- hypothesis risk

## Inputs required

- Plan/strategy/solution
- Known evidence
- Decision stakes
- Test constraints

## Workflow

1. Extract statements that must be true
2. Separate desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, and compliance assumptions
3. Score impact if false and uncertainty
4. Prioritize high-impact high-uncertainty items
5. Design smallest credible evidence-gathering test
6. Update status from evidence rather than confidence theater

## Outputs

- Assumption register
- Priority map
- Test proposals
- Evidence status

## Quality gates

- [ ] Assumptions are falsifiable
- [ ] Priority uses impact and uncertainty
- [ ] Tests can change a decision
- [ ] Evidence updates status
