# Competitor Analysis

## Purpose

Map direct and adjacent competitors, their positioning, strengths, weaknesses, pricing, GTM, customer signals, threats, and differentiation opportunities.

## When to activate

- competitor analysis
- competitive landscape
- competitive research
- differentiation opportunity
- battlecard
- compare competitors

## Inputs required

- Product/market definition
- Target segment/geography
- Known competitors
- Customer evidence
- Current date/freshness needs

## Workflow

1. Define market scope and direct-vs-adjacent criteria.
2. Identify primary competitors from current evidence.
3. Research official product/pricing pages and independent customer/market sources.
4. Compare positioning, capabilities, pricing, GTM, strengths, weaknesses, and switching costs.
5. Separate observed fact from inference.
6. Map unmet jobs, underserved segments, and differentiation opportunities.
7. Produce monitoring list for emerging threats.

## Outputs

- Competitive set
- Competitor profiles
- Comparison matrix
- Differentiation opportunities
- Positioning recommendation
- Evidence gaps

## Quality gates

- [ ] Current claims require current sources
- [ ] No market-share estimate presented as fact without evidence
- [ ] Direct and indirect alternatives separated
- [ ] At least two source types for material conclusions where possible

## Road signs

- When **customer language** dominates → go to **Customer Research**.
- When **strategy** dominates → go to **Product Strategy**.
- When **pricing** dominates → go to **Pricing Strategy**.
