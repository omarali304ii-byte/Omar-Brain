# Market Sizing

## Purpose

Estimate TAM, SAM, and SOM using top-down and bottom-up triangulation with explicit assumptions and confidence.

## When to activate

- market sizing
- tam sam som
- addressable market
- market opportunity
- size this market

## Inputs required

- Market definition
- Geography
- Customer segment
- Pricing/value metric
- Channel/product constraints
- Current market data

## Workflow

1. Define market boundaries first
2. Build top-down estimate from current sourced data
3. Build bottom-up estimate from units × price × frequency
4. Reconcile differences instead of averaging blindly
5. Scope SAM from real service constraints
6. Estimate SOM from capacity, competition, and time horizon
7. List assumptions, confidence, and validation plan

## Outputs

- TAM/SAM/SOM table
- Top-down and bottom-up models
- Assumption register
- Confidence/risk notes

## Quality gates

- [ ] Current data is cited
- [ ] Top-down and bottom-up both exist
- [ ] Estimates are labeled separately from facts
- [ ] SOM reflects go-to-market capacity
