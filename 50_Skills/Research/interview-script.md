# Interview Script

## Purpose

Design non-leading customer/user interviews that uncover real behavior, triggers, alternatives, and outcomes.

## When to activate

- interview script
- customer interview questions
- user interview
- research interview

## Inputs required

- Research objective
- Target participant
- Known hypotheses
- Time limit
- Ethical/privacy constraints

## Workflow

1. Define what decision the interview must inform
2. Start with recent concrete behavior, not opinions about hypothetical futures
3. Ask for chronology, trigger, alternatives, constraints, and outcome
4. Use neutral follow-ups and silence
5. Avoid pitching or teaching during research
6. Include consent and recording boundaries
7. Plan synthesis fields before interviewing

## Outputs

- Interview guide
- Opening/consent script
- Core questions
- Probe bank
- Synthesis fields

## Quality gates

- [ ] Questions are non-leading
- [ ] Recent behavior is prioritized
- [ ] Research objective maps to questions
- [ ] Sensitive data handling is explicit
