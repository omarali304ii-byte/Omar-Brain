# Opportunity Solution Tree

## Purpose

Connect a measurable outcome to evidence-backed opportunities, candidate solutions, and experiments without jumping straight to features.

## When to activate

- opportunity solution tree
- opportunity tree
- product discovery tree
- map opportunities

## Inputs required

- Target outcome
- Research evidence
- Known opportunities
- Candidate solutions
- Constraints

## Workflow

1. Define one measurable outcome
2. Extract opportunities from evidence in customer language
3. Structure opportunities without forcing false hierarchy
4. Generate multiple solutions per opportunity
5. Attach experiments to risky assumptions
6. Prioritize by outcome impact, evidence, and uncertainty
7. Keep rejected branches and reasoning

## Outputs

- Opportunity tree
- Evidence links
- Solution options
- Experiment candidates

## Quality gates

- [ ] Outcome is measurable
- [ ] Opportunities come from evidence
- [ ] Multiple solutions exist for important opportunities
- [ ] Experiments test assumptions
