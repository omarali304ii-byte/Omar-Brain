# Pricing Strategy

## Purpose

Design pricing and packaging that align price with customer value, market alternatives, GTM motion, and business goals.

## When to activate

- pricing
- pricing tiers
- freemium
- free trial
- packaging
- price increase
- value metric
- willingness to pay
- monetization
- how much should i charge

## Inputs required

- Business/product type
- Target market and GTM
- Current pricing/performance
- Value delivered
- Competitor alternatives
- Pricing goal

## Workflow

1. Separate packaging, value metric, and price point.
2. Map customer value and next-best alternatives.
3. Choose a value metric that scales with value received.
4. Design clear tier differentiation and limits.
5. Use willingness-to-pay research where material.
6. Model monthly/annual and enterprise/custom implications.
7. Check conversion, ARPU, churn, unit economics, and channel fit.
8. Define experiments and migration/grandfathering for changes.

## Outputs

- Pricing architecture
- Tier/packaging matrix
- Value metric rationale
- Research plan
- Change/experiment plan

## Quality gates

- [ ] No price chosen only from cost-plus logic
- [ ] No fake precision without willingness-to-pay evidence
- [ ] Tier differences must be understandable
- [ ] Pricing changes include customer migration/communication plan

## Road signs

- When **customer evidence** dominates → go to **Customer Research**.
- When **competitive prices** dominates → go to **Competitor Analysis**.
- When **pricing page** dominates → go to **Page CRO**.
