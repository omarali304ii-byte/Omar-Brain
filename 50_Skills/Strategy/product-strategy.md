# Product Strategy

## Purpose

Create a coherent product strategy across vision, segments, costs, value propositions, trade-offs, metrics, growth, capabilities, and defensibility.

## When to activate

- product strategy
- strategy canvas
- product direction
- strategic plan
- north star metric
- defensibility

## Inputs required

- Product and current positioning
- Market/customer evidence
- Competitors
- Resources/constraints
- Business priorities

## Workflow

1. Define vision and desired impact.
2. Choose problem-based target segments and first beachhead.
3. Clarify relative cost position.
4. Create segment-specific value propositions.
5. Write explicit trade-offs and what will not be done.
6. Define North Star and near-term OMTM.
7. Define growth motion and channels.
8. Identify required capabilities and build/partner choices.
9. Test defensibility and copyability.
10. Surface critical hypotheses and low-cost validation experiments.

## Outputs

- Product Strategy Canvas
- Trade-off list
- Metrics
- Capability plan
- Hypothesis/experiment register

## Quality gates

- [ ] All strategy elements must reinforce rather than contradict each other
- [ ] No demographic-only segment definition
- [ ] Explicit non-goals required
- [ ] Critical assumptions labeled and testable

## Road signs

- When **customer evidence** dominates → go to **Customer Research**.
- When **execution spec** dominates → go to **Create PRD**.
- When **value articulation** dominates → go to **Value Proposition**.
