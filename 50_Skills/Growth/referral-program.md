# Referral Program

## Purpose

Design referral loops where incentive, timing, eligibility, fraud controls, and economics support sustainable acquisition.

## When to activate

- referral program
- refer a friend
- affiliate referral
- word of mouth loop
- customer referral

## Inputs required

- Product value moment
- Customer segments
- Reward economics
- Fraud risk
- Tracking capability

## Workflow

1. Identify who has earned the right to refer and when
2. Define referrer/referee value
3. Model unit economics and caps
4. Design attribution and anti-abuse rules
5. Choose trigger moments and channels
6. Pilot with a segment
7. Measure incremental referred customers and quality

## Outputs

- Referral mechanic
- Eligibility/reward rules
- Fraud controls
- Experiment/measurement plan

## Quality gates

- [ ] Economics are modeled
- [ ] Fraud/abuse path reviewed
- [ ] Incrementality is considered
- [ ] Referral ask follows value
