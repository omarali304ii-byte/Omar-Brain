# Churn Prevention

## Purpose

Reduce preventable churn by segmenting causes, identifying leading signals, improving value delivery, and testing targeted interventions.

## When to activate

- churn prevention
- reduce churn
- retention
- customers cancelling
- save offer
- win back

## Inputs required

- Churn definition
- Segments/cohorts
- Usage/support/billing data
- Exit feedback
- Unit economics

## Workflow

1. Separate voluntary/involuntary and segment-specific churn
2. Quantify when and where churn occurs
3. Combine behavior with qualitative reasons
4. Identify leading signals and causal hypotheses
5. Design intervention matched to cause
6. Protect against discounting away bad fit
7. Measure retained value, not just cancelled cancellations

## Outputs

- Churn diagnosis
- Risk segments/signals
- Intervention backlog
- Measurement plan

## Quality gates

- [ ] Churn causes are segmented
- [ ] Intervention maps to a cause
- [ ] Retention quality is measured
- [ ] Dark-pattern cancellation is forbidden
