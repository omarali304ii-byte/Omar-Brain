# Email Sequence

## Purpose

Design multi-email lifecycle flows where each email has one job and the sequence moves the audience toward a measurable outcome.

## When to activate

- email sequence
- drip campaign
- welcome sequence
- nurture sequence
- onboarding emails
- re-engagement emails
- lifecycle email
- email automation

## Inputs required

- Sequence type
- Audience entry trigger
- Current awareness/relationship
- Primary conversion goal
- Success metric and constraints

## Workflow

1. Define the trigger and exit condition
2. Map one job per email and one primary CTA
3. Sequence value before asks and avoid redundant messages
4. Choose cadence from sales cycle, urgency, and audience behavior
5. Write subject, preview, hook, value, CTA, and next-state expectation
6. Instrument opens/clicks/conversions and suppression rules
7. Review after data; do not optimize on opens alone

## Outputs

- Sequence map
- Email briefs or drafts
- Timing/cadence
- Trigger/exit rules
- Measurement plan

## Quality gates

- [ ] Every email has one job
- [ ] Sequence has an exit condition
- [ ] No duplicate messages with different subject lines
- [ ] Metrics connect to business outcome
