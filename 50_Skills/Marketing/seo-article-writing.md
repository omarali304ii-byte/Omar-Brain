# SEO Article Writing

## Purpose

Create long-form, search-intent-aligned articles with evidence, useful structure, internal linking, metadata, FAQ opportunities, and readability checks.

## When to activate

- seo article
- yoast ready article
- long form seo
- write article for keyword
- seo content

## Inputs required

- Primary keyword/topic
- Search intent
- Target market/language
- Client/service context
- Source/evidence pack
- Internal-link targets

## Workflow

1. Confirm intent and audience before drafting.
2. Research current SERP/topic evidence when freshness matters.
3. Build a differentiated H1/H2/H3 structure.
4. Use the target keyphrase naturally in high-value locations without stuffing.
5. Write for usefulness and complete task satisfaction, not arbitrary word count.
6. Add source-backed claims, internal links, and FAQ only where useful.
7. Create title/meta suggestions and schema opportunities.
8. Run factual, readability, duplication, language/script, and client-voice checks.

## Outputs

- Publish-ready article
- SEO title/meta
- Internal-link map
- FAQ/schema candidates
- Evidence/assumption notes

## Quality gates

- [ ] No fabricated facts or statistics
- [ ] No keyword stuffing
- [ ] No forced minimum length when intent is satisfied sooner
- [ ] Claims requiring evidence remain sourced or qualified

## Road signs

- When **topic/customer research** dominates → go to **Customer Research**.
- When **conversion goal** dominates → go to **Page CRO**.
