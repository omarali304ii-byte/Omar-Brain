# Social Content

## Purpose

Create platform-aware social content tied to a clear audience, message pillar, campaign goal, and measurable next action.

## When to activate

- social content
- social media post
- linkedin post
- instagram content
- content calendar
- social strategy
- social post

## Inputs required

- Audience
- Platform
- Brand voice
- Goal
- Content pillars
- Offer/campaign context

## Workflow

1. Choose one audience tension or insight
2. Match format to platform behavior instead of cross-posting blindly
3. Create a strong opening hook
4. Deliver one coherent idea with proof/example
5. Use CTA appropriate to awareness stage
6. Repurpose only after adapting context and format
7. Track saves, qualified engagement, clicks, and downstream outcomes

## Outputs

- Post drafts
- Content angles
- Platform adaptations
- Measurement notes

## Quality gates

- [ ] Platform fit is explicit
- [ ] No generic engagement bait
- [ ] Claims remain evidence-backed
- [ ] CTA matches funnel stage
