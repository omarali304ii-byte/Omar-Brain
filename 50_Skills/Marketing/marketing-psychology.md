# Marketing Psychology

## Purpose

Apply behavioral principles to reduce friction and improve comprehension without manipulation, dark patterns, or invented scarcity.

## When to activate

- marketing psychology
- behavioral psychology
- persuasion
- cognitive bias
- social proof
- scarcity
- framing

## Inputs required

- Audience decision
- Current friction
- Offer
- Evidence
- Ethical constraints

## Workflow

1. Identify the real decision and uncertainty
2. Map relevant behavioral mechanisms such as social proof, loss aversion, defaults, anchoring, and cognitive load
3. Select the smallest mechanism that addresses observed friction
4. Implement transparently
5. Test behavior and guard against harm or deceptive pressure

## Outputs

- Behavioral hypothesis
- Ethical intervention
- Test plan
- Risk notes
