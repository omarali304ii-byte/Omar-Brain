# Cold Email

## Purpose

Create concise, relevant outbound email that earns attention without pretending personalization or making unsupported claims.

## When to activate

- cold email
- outbound email
- sales outreach
- prospecting email
- cold outreach
- email prospect

## Inputs required

- ICP and recipient role
- Trigger/reason for contact
- Relevant problem
- Credible proof
- Desired next step

## Workflow

1. Research only enough to establish real relevance
2. Lead with a specific reason for contact
3. Connect observed context to one plausible problem without overclaiming
4. Offer one concise value hypothesis and proof point
5. Use a low-friction CTA
6. Create follow-ups that add new value instead of repeating the first email
7. Check compliance and opt-out requirements for target jurisdiction

## Outputs

- Initial email
- 2-4 follow-ups
- Subject options
- Personalization data requirements

## Quality gates

- [ ] No fake personalization
- [ ] No fabricated familiarity or proof
- [ ] One CTA per email
- [ ] Message is readable on mobile
