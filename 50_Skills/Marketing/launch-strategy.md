# Launch Strategy

## Purpose

Plan staged launches that combine controlled learning, owned/rented/borrowed channels, conversion capture, and post-launch follow-through.

## When to activate

- launch strategy
- product launch
- feature launch
- beta launch
- early access
- waitlist
- product hunt
- gtm launch
- release announcement

## Inputs required

- Product and release scope
- Audience and readiness
- Launch objective
- Channel assets
- Operational constraints
- Production readiness status

## Workflow

1. Verify release is allowed to launch; do not replace production gates
2. Select launch phase: internal, alpha, beta, early access, full
3. Map owned, rented, and borrowed channels with owned capture paths
4. Define message, proof, audience, and CTA per phase
5. Create pre-launch, launch-day, and post-launch actions
6. Instrument acquisition, activation, conversion, and feedback
7. Feed launch evidence back into product and strategy

## Outputs

- Phased launch plan
- Channel map
- Asset checklist
- Measurement plan
- Post-launch loop

## Quality gates

- [ ] Launch phase matches product readiness
- [ ] Every rented/borrowed channel has a capture path
- [ ] Owners and success metrics are explicit
- [ ] Post-launch actions exist
