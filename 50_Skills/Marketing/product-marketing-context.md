# Product Marketing Context

## Purpose

Create a reusable context pack so marketing skills share the same audience, positioning, proof, voice, alternatives, and constraints.

## When to activate

- product marketing context
- marketing context
- positioning context
- brand context
- shared marketing context

## Inputs required

- Product facts
- Audience research
- Positioning
- Alternatives
- Proof
- Brand voice
- Constraints

## Workflow

1. Collect verified product facts and unknowns separately
2. Summarize ICP/JTBD and language from evidence
3. Record positioning, alternatives, proof, objections, and voice
4. Mark assumptions and staleness
5. Link source notes and related projects
6. Require downstream marketing skills to read this context first

## Outputs

- Reusable marketing context file
- Unknowns/gaps
- Source links
- Review date

## Quality gates

- [ ] Facts and assumptions are separated
- [ ] No invented persona details
- [ ] Sources/provenance exist
- [ ] Review date is explicit
