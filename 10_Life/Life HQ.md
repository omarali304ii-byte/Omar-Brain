# Life HQ

## Purpose
Long-running personal systems and direction. Time-bounded outcomes should become goals or projects rather than endless life notes.

## Sections
- `Vision`: long-term direction and principles.
- `Areas`: ongoing responsibilities such as health or personal finance.
- `Goals`: measurable outcomes.
- `Routines`: repeatable personal systems.

## Rules
- Daily journaling belongs in `80_Reviews/Daily`.
- A finite personal outcome belongs in `40_Projects` when it requires coordinated work.
- Sensitive personal material should use `ai_access: restricted` or `denied`.

## AI Road Signs

**You are here:** ongoing personal life systems.

| When the destination is... | Go to... |
|---|---|
| finite multi-step personal outcome | [[40_Projects/Projects HQ]] |
| daily/weekly/monthly reflection | [[80_Reviews/Reviews HQ]] |
| capability improvement | [[50_Skills/Skills HQ]] |
| sensitive relationship context | [[70_People/People HQ]] |

**Do not stay here when:** the outcome has a finite done definition.  
**Arrival proof:** canonical area/goal/routine or a resolved project with next action.
