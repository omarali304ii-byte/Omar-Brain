# Evidence Pack Standard

A completion evidence pack links claims to proof.

## Required
- scope/acceptance criteria,
- revision or artifact identity,
- commands/actions run,
- results,
- failed checks and resolution,
- manual/runtime checks,
- known limitations,
- final gate status.

## Principle
The evidence pack should let another agent or Omar verify why “done” was justified without trusting the author's confidence.
