# Self-Improvement Guardrails

AI may improve future behavior by writing evidence-backed learning. It may not perform uncontrolled self-modification.

## AI may
- create candidate lessons,
- create failure signatures,
- update evidence counts,
- propose patterns,
- propose standard changes,
- mark contradictions,
- deprecate only when authority/evidence supports and policy allows.

## AI may not silently
- alter Constitution,
- alter security/access policy,
- alter stop conditions,
- add top-level taxonomy,
- promote itself to trusted source,
- erase failures that make a pattern look bad,
- rewrite success metrics,
- approve its own global standard proposal.

The learning system must improve accuracy, not merely confidence.
