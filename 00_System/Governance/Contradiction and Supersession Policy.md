# Contradiction and Supersession Policy

## Never silently overwrite
When new information conflicts with durable memory:
1. preserve both claims,
2. link evidence,
3. identify authority and freshness,
4. mark uncertainty,
5. resolve or escalate,
6. use `supersedes` / `superseded_by` when resolved.

## Current truth update
A canonical current-state note may be updated only when the new claim is better supported than the old claim for the relevant truth question.

## Deprecated knowledge
Deprecated items remain searchable but must clearly point to the replacement. Retrieval should down-rank deprecated/superseded notes.
