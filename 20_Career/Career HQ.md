# Career HQ

## Purpose
Professional direction and employment journey.

## Canonical sections
- `Strategy`: target roles, positioning, long-term direction.
- `Roles`: current and historical professional roles.
- `Opportunities`: live job/freelance opportunities.
- `Portfolio`: evidence of ability.
- `CV`: canonical CV material and variants.

## Boundary
- Learning capability belongs in `50_Skills`.
- Time-bounded portfolio builds belong in `40_Projects`.
- Company-building belongs in `30_Business`.
