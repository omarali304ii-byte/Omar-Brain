# People HQ

Person notes exist for relationship context that is genuinely useful, not surveillance.

## Rules
- Keep respectful, relevant context only.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive attributes.
- Default to `ai_access: restricted`.
- Link meetings, companies, projects, and decisions rather than copying histories.
