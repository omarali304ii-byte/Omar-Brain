# Business HQ

## Purpose
Ongoing business entities and operating systems.

## Canonical sections
- `Organizations`: companies/brands.
- `Products`: ongoing products and services.
- `Clients`: client entities and relationship context.
- `Strategy`: durable strategic thinking.
- `Operations`: repeatable business processes.
- `Offers`: commercial offers/packages.

## Boundary
A finite launch, migration, integration, campaign, or build belongs in `40_Projects` and links back to the relevant organization/product/client.
