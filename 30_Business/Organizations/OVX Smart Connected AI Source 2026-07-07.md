## Source Boundary
This is a user-declared company vision and operating philosophy for OVX. It is authoritative for Omar's intended company direction, brand language, and design doctrine.

It is not, by itself, proof that OVX Core exists in production, that client ecosystems are already connected, or that every OVX project currently satisfies the standard. Those claims require project, repository, runtime, client, and operational evidence.

## Durable Claims Extracted
- OVX should build connected intelligent ecosystems, not isolated software.
- Every OVX solution should be powerful alone and more valuable together.
- A client should not need to restart digitally from zero after joining OVX.
- The strongest strategic doctrine is: One Reality. Shared Memory. Compounding Intelligence.
- OVX should be context-engine-first, not merely RAG-first.
- RAG is useful but not sufficient for business intelligence.
- Structured truth, analytics, event history, relationship graphs, knowledge retrieval, permissions, and AI context preparation all have different roles.
- The client's data belongs to the client.
- Permissions, tenant boundaries, least privilege, classification, audit, and human approval are part of the intelligence architecture.
- Every meaningful OVX project should ask what it connects to, what it contributes, and how it makes future OVX systems stronger.

## Explicit Non-Claims
- This source does not prove current implementation quality.
- This source does not prove production readiness.
- This source does not prove client adoption.
- This source does not prove that existing OVX projects already follow the doctrine.
- This source does not authorize mixing client data across tenants or using client-owned data as OVX-owned training material.
