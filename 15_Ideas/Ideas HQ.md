# Ideas HQ

Ideas are possibilities, not commitments. Keep them out of active project views until validated and promoted.

## Lifecycle
`candidate` → `validating` → `validated` → `promoted` or `rejected`

## Rules
- one idea per durable idea note,
- link related projects, products, companies, skills, and problems,
- record why it matters and the next validation step,
- do not create a project until an outcome and done definition exist,
- when promoted, link the new project and preserve idea history.
