# Inbox

This is a **processing queue**, not permanent storage.

## Capture rule
When you do not know where something belongs, put it here immediately. Zero friction beats forced classification.

## Processing rule
For each item:
1. preserve source,
2. classify purpose,
3. search duplicates,
4. choose create/update/merge/link/inbox/discard,
5. route,
6. verify.

See [[00_System/Taxonomy and Routing Rules]] and [[00_System/AI/Brain Router Prompt]].
